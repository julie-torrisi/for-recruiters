>How I work with AI

Teach the machine the rules.

AI is a brilliant new hire, so I onboard it first: house rules, the design system and a short brief. I prototype in the code the product ships in. A person approves before anything ships.

>Since Meta

An intentional dream reset.

I left Meta to take a sabbatical I'd been planning, and the timing turned out to be perfect. I did a real reset, including a complete social media detox, which I highly recommend. My partner and I took our two kids to Spain, Switzerland and Italy, and then Paris.

Somewhere in there, we realized we didn't want to live in New York anymore. So we made a big move: we sold our house and moved two kids and two dogs down the East Coast to Apex, North Carolina, in the Research Triangle. There are still a few boxes to unpack, but we love it here.

I kept building, too: Cadence, my design kit with tokens in Figma synced to code in GitHub, and Tidy, a free Figma file template on Figma Community.

I came back with a lot of energy and a clear picture of the work I want next: taking a product that's grown complex and making it simple, with a system other teams want to build on. I also know the kind of place I want to do it. I believe in staying curious, and in building a team where people feel free to think critically and ask, "Are we solving the right problem? Are we asking the right questions?" Those are the teams I do my best work on, and where I see my future.
